- Per-ticker price/volume history is split across many tables. `stockmarket_stocktrade_all` is a stored union of only the 2,715 tickers whose native Volume is bigint. There are 38 additional individual `stockmarket_<ticker>` tables (lowercase ticker, e.g. `stockmarket_sdow`, `stockmarket_ftft`, `stockmarket_ftr`, `stockmarket_adp`, `stockmarket_cmi`, `stockmarket_cma`) whose native Volume is double precision and are NOT included in `stocktrade_all`. Any question touching "all"/"every" ticker's prices or volume (max/min/avg price, existence checks over dates, etc.) must union `stocktrade_all` with these 38 per-ticker tables (cast Volume to a common type) or matching rows are silently dropped. Compare `lower(stockinfo."Symbol")` against the union's ticker column to find which symbols need their own table.
- "Company Description" in `stockmarket_stockinfo` is one free-text sentence: the company name leads, followed by a verb phrase (offers/is/provides/specializes/operates/focuses/invests/based/manages/empowers/revolutionizes/utilizes/develops/generates/excels/partners/owns/manufactures/delivers/crafts/connects/managed/presents/engages/harnesses/etc.) or a ", <lowercase clause>". Cut the string right before that first verb word or before the comma+lowercase clause, then trim a trailing ", ". Also strip periods from corporate abbreviations (e.g. "Inc.", "Co.", "Ltd." -> "Inc", "Co", "Ltd") to get the canonical name.
- Never restrict stockinfo to whichever tickers happen to already be in `stocktrade_all` before joining/filtering; that silently excludes the 38 double-precision-volume tickers. Build the full unioned price source first, then apply filters.
- Financial Status codes: D=deficient, E=delinquent, H=deficient+delinquent (both) — these three count as "financially troubled". G/J/K also involve bankruptcy and should be excluded unless bankruptcy is explicitly asked about.
