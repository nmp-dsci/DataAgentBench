- There is NO category column. World / Sports / Business / Science/Technology must be inferred from `agnews_articles.title` + `description` (the AG News corpus). `doc` is just a JSON copy of those two fields.
- Joins are clean and 1:1: `agnews_article_metadata.article_id` covers all 127,600 articles; `agnews_authors.name` is unique, so filtering on the exact name is safe.
- `publication_date` is varchar, always 'YYYY-MM-DD', so string range comparisons work. Dates span 2004-01-01 to 2022-12-30 in every region.
- `region` has 5 continent values, each with about 25.4k–25.7k rows. Region totals are nearly equal, so a ranking of regions depends on classifying the category well. Return every region with its count, not only the top one.
- Category signals: a source tag in parentheses at the end of the title (about 18k titles have one). PC World, SPACE.com, Ziff Davis, InfoWorld, MacCentral, NewsFactor, TechWeb and SiliconValley.com mean Science/Technology. Forbes.com, Investor's Business Daily, TheDeal.com, The Motley Fool and FT.com mean Business. AP, Reuters, AFP, Canadian Press and UpdateN appear in every category and tell you nothing.
- Science/Technology covers computing, internet, software, telecom, space and science. A tech company's earnings or merger story is usually Business. Sports covers games, teams, athletes and the Olympics. World covers politics, conflict, diplomacy and disasters.
- For a small set of rows (for example one author's articles), SELECT every title+description, classify each row by meaning, and answer in derived mode. Keep a careful tally and check that the four category counts add up to the total.
- "Per year from Y1 to Y2 inclusive": divide by the number of calendar years (Y2−Y1+1). The window is `>= 'Y1-01-01'` and `< '(Y2+1)-01-01'`.
