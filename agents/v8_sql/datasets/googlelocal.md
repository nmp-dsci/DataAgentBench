- Business trades (massage, spa, salon, etc.) are often not named literally in `googlelocal_business_description.name`; `description` is paraphrased, so the same trade can appear under synonyms. Search both `name` and `description` with a synonym-aware regex, then check the pattern against all 79 rows (look at what it matches and at close misses) before you trust it.
- Join `googlelocal_review` to `googlelocal_business_description` only on `gmap_id`. Names can repeat or differ in casing.
- `googlelocal_review.rating` is an integer from 1 to 5, so averages are often whole numbers. Filter and sort on the full-precision average and round only for display.
- `googlelocal_review.time` mixes several text layouts, e.g. "September 03, 2020 at 04:15 PM", "2021-04-12 17:07:52", "08 Mar 2020, 00:04". A match on the 4-digit year works for all of them. Reading the month or day, or casting to a timestamp, needs a separate parse for each layout.
- `hours`: NULL for 13 of the 79 businesses. For the rest it is JSON text of 7 [day, value] pairs, starting on Thursday. Each value is 'Closed', 'Open 24 hours', or a range 'open–close' with an en dash. The close time always has AM/PM. The open time may omit it and then takes the close's (e.g. '5–11PM'). Minutes are optional ('6:30AM–5PM'). A close at or before the open means the business runs past midnight.
- `state` is the live status text (e.g. 'Open ⋅ Closes 5PM', 'Permanently closed', 'Temporarily closed'), not a US state. The location (city, state) appears only in `description`.
