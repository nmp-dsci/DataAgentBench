- Per-ticker price/volume history is split: `stockmarket_stocktrade_all` (union of most tickers, key `_table` = lowercase symbol, Volume bigint) PLUS 38 separate `stockmarket_<ticker>` tables (lowercase ticker as table name, e.g. `stockmarket_adp`, `stockmarket_cmi`) whose Volume is double precision and excluded from `stocktrade_all`. `stockmarket_stockinfo` has 2,752 Symbols but `stocktrade_all` covers only 2,715 distinct `_table` values. For questions needing "every" stock's prices/volume, check which target symbols are absent from `stocktrade_all` (compare `lower(stockinfo."Symbol")` to `stocktrade_all._table`) and UNION in matching per-ticker tables, or rows get silently dropped.
- Join stockinfo to trade data on ticker symbol (`lower("Symbol") = lower(_table)`, or the matching per-ticker table); never assume one table holds all trading data.
- "Company Description" is one free-text sentence starting with the name followed by a varying verb phrase (offers, is, provides, specializes, operates, focuses, invests, based, manages, develops, owns, manufactures, delivers, engages, harnesses, etc.) or a ", <lowercase clause>,". Trim right before that phrase for the "name", then strip trailing ", " and stray periods. Never return the raw sentence as "the name".
- Dates in price tables are text 'YYYY-MM-DD'; `LIKE 'YYYY%'` selects a year.
- "List X and report the total count" wants one result: rows plus a final labeled total row, not a scalar.
