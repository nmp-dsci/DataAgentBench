04 · plan-first (D34) · traces

The plan object's shape trips Haiku and Sonnet; Opus sends it right every time

v2_sql · Haiku, no plan

0 of 54 trials with an unparsed submit_answer; 55 submit calls; 2 never submitted.

v3_sql · Haiku + plan

43 of 54 trials with an unparsed submit; 123 submit calls; 5 answered in prose without submitting, 2 more ran out of the 30 turns. No SQL: 7 of 54.

v4_sql · Sonnet + plan

26 of 54 trials with an unparsed submit; 84 submit calls; every trial recovered and submitted.

v5_sql · Opus + plan

0 of 54 unparsed; 54 submit calls, one per trial; plan recorded 54 of 54; median 5 turns.

Of the 76 unparsed calls in v3, the 39 fully visible in the trace fail at the same place: "plan": followed by seven plain lines (sources: …) where the schema wants an object with seven keys. The other 37 are cut at 2,048 characters by the trace's span trimming. The retries cost turns and money, and for Haiku whole answers; part of Sonnet's extra cost over Opus ($6.59 against $5.04) is 30 extra submit calls.

Fix to decide: take plan as one string of seven labelled lines (what the smaller models write anyway) and parse it in read_plan, or accept both shapes. It matters for Haiku and Sonnet, not for Opus.

07 · what changed on the way from the s08 plan

Ten departures, each recorded where it happened

  • Section cap 282 → 600. A 5,000-char system.md cap left 282 chars a section; raised to 8,000 on request and the round re-run. v3_sql's system.md is 6,573 chars.
  • The rationale is leak-checked only. The guard had length-capped the optimiser's rationale; 2 of 33 refusals came from that. Now only leaks refuse a rationale (tests/test_optimise_guard.py).
  • stockindex has no notes. Two leak refusals dropped them, as the guard's rule says; it had none in v2_sql either.
  • The reader writes the agent's lines and the verdicts in one call per failed statement, not two.
  • The section tool is write_section; dataset sessions keep write_notes.
  • dab version-model SRC --into NEW --model M made v4_sql and v5_sql: copies with only the model changed and measured_against set.
  • Round 1 had 9 leak refusals, not 11. s08 counted 11 leak problems spread over 9 refusals; the tab counts refusals.
  • v5_sql (added on request). The opus alias now resolves to claude-opus-5-5 (was claude-opus-5).
  • Agent SDK 0.2.157 → 0.2.159, for a bundled CLI (2.1.281) that accepts Opus 5.5.
  • Isolation for CLI 2.1.281. Its new built-in telemetry plugin is switched off as agents-md is; the account's organisation id (credential_org) joins the email on the allowlist, since the CLI has no switch for it. dab isolation-check passes on v5_sql.
